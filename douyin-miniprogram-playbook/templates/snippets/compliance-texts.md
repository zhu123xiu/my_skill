# Compliance copy snippets

Use these as starting points and confirm the current platform requirements before submission.

- Payment notice: Payment success is subject to platform confirmation. If a refund is needed, contact customer service through the published support channel.
- iOS purchase block (canPurchase reason): 「iOS 暂不支持购买，请使用激活码」— 苹果侧虚拟支付限制，端上拦截后引导用户走激活码兑换；提审前按平台最新要求核对文案。
- Service notice: Describe the digital service, delivery timing, refund boundary, and support contact in plain language.
- Privacy notice: Collect only the fields required for the stated feature and link to the full privacy policy from the app entry points.
